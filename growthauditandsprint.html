Growth Audit & Sprint

SURGICAL DIAGNOSTICS & BUDGET LEAK RECOVERY IN 30 DAYS

What we do

Are your current digital campaigns underperforming, quietly draining your monthly advertising budget without driving predictable, scalable revenue? At Bixal Media, we step in with a rigorous, data-driven methodology to diagnose the precise health of your digital ecosystem.

Over a focused 15 to 30-day sprint, we conduct a deep-dive audit across your Google, Microsoft and Meta Ads accounts, conversion funnels, and web analytics (GA4). By leveraging advanced AI-driven diagnostic tools alongside our senior strategic oversight, we do more than just point out superficial flaws. We uncover hidden budget leaks, structural tracking errors, and friction points in your user journey that traditional manual reviews routinely miss.

The Deliverable

You receive an exhaustive diagnostic report and a clear, actionable 3-step roadmap designed to immediately recover wasted spend and re-engineer your performance. It is the ultimate low-friction entry point to establish technical authority, correct your trajectory, and set the foundation for long-term growth.
